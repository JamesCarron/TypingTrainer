Choosing three of five — the sans faces, side by side

All five shortlisted faces at the settings actually chosen for the tool: 17px, five lines, 0.6 fade per line, the current line at 1.32×, errors shown as the glyph in red. A face that looks handsome on a specimen sheet can still be wrong at 17px with a 60%-faded neighbour above it, so the first section is the only one that really decides anything — the other two are there to explain why.
